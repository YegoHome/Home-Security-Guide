# Contributing

Thank you for helping make this guide better.

## How changes work

- The original guide is changed only by the maintainer.
- You can fork (copy) the project and change your copy freely.
- To suggest a change to the original, open an issue or a pull request. The maintainer reviews every suggestion before anything is merged.

## What makes a good suggestion

1. Name the part and page number (shown at the bottom of every page, for example "Part 2 · Page 4 of 13").
2. Describe what you expected and what you saw. Include your Windows 11 edition and version (`winver`).
3. Link an official source (Microsoft, Intel, Oracle, or the tool's vendor) for any factual change. Suggestions without a source can't be merged.

## Style rules

- Keep the house metaphor consistent. Terms are defined once in the Key Ring (`assets/guide.js`) and mean the same thing everywhere.
- Read-only checks come before changes. Every change must include how to undo it and how to verify it worked.
- Plain language for beginners: short sentences, one action per step.
